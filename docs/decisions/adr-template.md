# ADR-NNN: <Short Decision Title>

Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-XXX
Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What problem are we solving? What forces (technical, team, timeline) are in play? What options
were on the table?

## Decision

What did we decide, stated as a single clear sentence, followed by the reasoning.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result. Include operational costs (what we now have to
monitor/maintain), not just upside.

## Alternatives Considered

Each alternative gets one line: what it was and why it was rejected. Don't omit the option that
seems obvious in hindsight — that's exactly the one a future reader will ask about.

## Links

Any specs, docs, or external references the decision leans on.
